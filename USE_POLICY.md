# Responsible use

This project supports noncommercial personal study and other uses permitted by [LICENSE](LICENSE), with authorization from the relevant institution and content owners.

The project does not approve, support or accept contributions intended for:

- Unauthorized access to accounts, courses, files or personal data; sharing, collecting or trading other people's credentials.
- Bypassing login controls, disabled access, rate limits, publication locks or institutional restrictions. A session cookie is not permission to circumvent a restriction.
- Selling, paywalling or monetizing the tool outside the rights granted in LICENSE, or redistributing course materials without the necessary rights.
- Impersonation, surveillance, harvesting student information, harassment, fraud or facilitating academic misconduct.
- Automatically submitting assignments, answering assessments, altering grades or hiding automation from the institution.

Respect the applicable service terms, institution rules, copyright and privacy obligations. Access to a file does not automatically grant redistribution rights. Seek institution-approved API/OAuth access when uncertain. Do not upload private or copyrighted material to an AI service without permission.

These expectations guide the project's design, support and contribution decisions. They do not amend the standardized PolyForm license or guarantee legal enforcement against every unethical use. The maintainers cannot monitor every deployment or promise that third parties will comply. Commercial rights are controlled by LICENSE; independent legal obligations continue to apply.
